About the project

For sale: an aparthotel project with 12 apartments in the coastal district of Albufereta. Playa de la Albufereta is about a 7-minute walk away.

The project converts an existing villa and hands it over turnkey: fully finished, furnished and equipped, with landscaped shared areas.

The aparthotel format serves different kinds of demand, from short coastal breaks to longer stays. For the buyer, turnkey delivery means the project itself covers the finishes, purchase of furniture and appliances, delivery and installation. This makes it possible to know in advance which costs are included in the purchase price and which belong in the launch budget.

What the project includes

Units12 apartments on two levels

Price and terms

Asking price €2,350,000. Across 12 apartments that is about €195,833 per unit. The figure includes the property with its shared areas and equipment and serves as a benchmark against other hospitality offers.

The developer is ready to sign a preliminary arras contract conditional on obtaining all required licences and permits.

Before paying the deposit, agree on the list of documents, when they will be obtained, the deposit refund terms and the handover procedure. The contract should also fix the handover date, the furnishing package and the works to be completed by then.
