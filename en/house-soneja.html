About the house

A 216 m² house on three floors of 72 m² each. Three façades bring plenty of light and cross-ventilation, and entrances from different streets make it easy to divide into separate apartments.

Original wooden beams, vaulted ceilings, stonework and old brick have survived inside and will be the highlight of the interior after renovation. The house needs work: façade, roof, utilities and a layout to suit the chosen use.

Soneja is a small town in the Alto Palancia, next to the Sierra de Espadán natural park. About 50 km to Valencia, around 20 minutes to Sagunto and the beaches, with its own commuter rail station to Valencia. Possible uses: a rural hotel, 3 to 6 apartments, a business on the ground floor with apartments above, or one large home. What is permitted is being confirmed with Soneja Town Hall; the owner has already filed a planning enquiry.
